<section class="cs-section">
  <div class="cs-section__label">Context</div>
  <h2 class="cs-section__title">A new law, no product to run it</h2>
  <div class="cs-prose">
    <p>The new public procurement law established Compra Ágil: a special modality initially for acquisitions up to 30 UTM, later extended to 100 UTM. The promise was simple — more speed, less bureaucracy. The stakes were high: 80% of all State purchases fall within this range, and no digital tool existed to operate the new modality. Someone had to design it from scratch.</p>
    <p>I led the design of that product: from strategic definition to interface components, including the flow architecture for both sides of the market — government agencies that buy and suppliers that quote.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-highlight-block">
    <p class="cs-highlight-block__question">The goal was not to digitize the existing process. It was to replace it.</p>
    <p class="cs-highlight-block__desc">Eliminate the technical requirements document. Remove intermediate administrative acts. Simplify the quotation process to a single step. If the flow is clear for both buyers and suppliers, cycle time drops and market participation grows.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="cs-section">
  <div class="cs-section__label">Process</div>
  <h2 class="cs-section__title">A structured approach to an undefined problem</h2>
  <div class="cs-prose">
    <p>Designing a product from scratch — with no precedent and a hard launch deadline tied to a new law — required a clear methodology. We followed a Double Diamond process: understand deeply before defining, define before prototyping, and build in a feedback loop from day one.</p>
  </div>
  <figure class="cs-figure cs-figure--clean">
    <img class="img-clean" src="/assets/method.png" alt="Double Diamond process: Discovery, Define, Develop, Delivery, Monitoring with Continuous Improvement" />
  </figure>
  <div class="cs-prose">
    <p>The product serves two very different users simultaneously. Every design decision had to work for both sides of the market without creating asymmetry or confusion.</p>
  </div>
  <figure class="cs-figure cs-figure--clean">
    <img class="img-clean" src="/assets/compra-agil-flujo.png" alt="Compra Ágil flow: buyer and supplier parallel journeys across 8 steps" />
  </figure>

<section class="cs-section">
  <div class="cs-section__label">Research</div>
  <h2 class="cs-section__title">Listening to both sides before designing anything</h2>
  <div class="cs-prose">
    <p>We ran more than 30 in-depth interviews with public agencies and suppliers — hospitals, regional institutions, municipalities, and small businesses — using qualitative exploratory methods to understand their real experience with public procurement before touching any interface.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-findings">
    <blockquote class="cs-finding">
      <p>"We have to run a full tender to buy printer paper. The process is the same as for an infrastructure project."</p>
      <cite>Procurement unit, public agency</cite>
    </blockquote>
    <blockquote class="cs-finding">
      <p>"As a small supplier I have no way of knowing if there are purchases in my sector. I find out through contacts, not through the platform."</p>
      <cite>Service provider, Metropolitan region</cite>
    </blockquote>
    <blockquote class="cs-finding">
      <p>"An urgent purchase can take weeks. By the time the order arrives, I've already solved the problem another way."</p>
      <cite>Head of supply, municipality</cite>
    </blockquote>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="cs-section">
  <div class="cs-section__label">Ideation</div>
  <h2 class="cs-section__title">Mapping what didn't exist yet</h2>
  <div class="cs-prose">
    <p>With the research findings clear, the work shifted to defining the product's architecture before any screens. Each step had to be validated before moving forward.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-timeline">
    <div class="cs-timeline__item">
      <div class="cs-timeline__marker"></div>
      <div class="cs-timeline__content">
        <h4>Regulatory scope definition</h4>
        <p>Reading and synthesis of the new law. Interviews with the legal team and procurement units to understand exactly what the law mandates and what remained as a design decision.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-timeline__item">
      <div class="cs-timeline__marker"></div>
      <div class="cs-timeline__content">
        <h4>Flow mapping for two profiles</h4>
        <p>The product has two sides: buyers who publish and suppliers who quote. I mapped both journeys separately before looking for convergence and divergence points.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-timeline__item">
      <div class="cs-timeline__marker"></div>
      <div class="cs-timeline__content">
        <h4>Status system architecture</h4>
        <p>Before any screen, I designed the complete taxonomy of order statuses. Without that structure, the system has no criterion — only appearance.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-timeline__item">
      <div class="cs-timeline__marker"></div>
      <div class="cs-timeline__content">
        <h4>Prototyping, validation and handoff</h4>
        <p>Iterative prototypes of the search interface, publishing form and order detail. Validation with real buyers from public agencies, then documentation for development.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="cs-section">
  <div class="cs-section__label">Solution Design</div>
  <h2 class="cs-section__title">A status system before any screen</h2>
  <div class="cs-prose">
    <p>Each status has its own meaning, semantic color and set of allowed actions. They are readable for both the buyer and the supplier equally — not just labels, but behavioral contracts that define what each user can do at every moment.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ca-states">
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--orden">Purchase order issued</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">The buyer selected a quote and issued the order. The supplier must accept within the deadline. Highest urgency status for the supplier.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--recibiendo">Published, receiving quotes</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">The purchase is active. Any enabled supplier can quote within the published deadline. Main opportunity status.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--segundo">Second call</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">There were not enough quotes in the first call. The deadline is extended, a second opportunity for suppliers.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--desierta">Deserted</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">No valid quotes were received. Terminal status, the buyer must start a new process.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--cancelada">Cancelled</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">The agency cancelled the purchase before completing the process. Can occur at any active stage.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--cerrada">Closed, awaiting results</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">The deadline ended. The buyer is evaluating proposals. Waiting status for the supplier.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-callout">
    <p><strong>Key design decision:</strong> statuses define what the user can do at each moment. The color, text and available actions change according to the status. The system has criterion, not just appearance.</p>
  </div>
</section>
