<section class="cs-section">
  <div class="cs-section__label">The challenge</div>
  <h2 class="cs-section__title">A new law, no product to run it</h2>
  <div class="cs-prose">
    <p>The new public procurement law established a special modality for acquisitions under 100 UTM: Compra Ágil. The promise was simple, more speed, less bureaucracy. The problem was that no digital tool existed to operate it. Someone had to design it from scratch.</p>
    <p>I led the design of that product: from strategic definition to interface components, including the flow architecture for both sides of the market, government agencies that buy and suppliers that quote.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-highlight-block">
    <p class="cs-highlight-block__question">The goal was not to digitize the existing process. It was to replace it.</p>
    <p class="cs-highlight-block__desc">If we create a dedicated flow for purchases under 100 UTM, with one-step publishing, clear statuses and visibility for both sides of the market, then we will reduce cycle time and increase supplier participation.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="cs-section">
  <div class="cs-section__label">The problem</div>

<section class="cs-section">
  <div class="cs-section__label">Process</div>
  <h2 class="cs-section__title">Designing both sides of the market at the same time</h2>
  <div class="cs-prose">
    <p>The project started with regulatory research, understanding exactly what the law allows and what remained as a design decision. From there, the process was iterative: define, prototype, validate and adjust before scaling.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-timeline">
    <div class="cs-timeline__item">
      <div class="cs-timeline__marker"></div>
      <div class="cs-timeline__content">
        <h4>Regulatory context research</h4>
        <p>Reading and synthesis of the new law. Interviews with the legal team and procurement units to understand the real scope, what the law allows and what remains as a design decision.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-timeline__item">
      <div class="cs-timeline__marker"></div>
      <div class="cs-timeline__content">
        <h4>Flow mapping for two profiles</h4>
        <p>The product has two sides: buyers who publish and suppliers who quote. I mapped both journeys separately before looking for convergence points.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-timeline__item">
      <div class="cs-timeline__marker"></div>
      <div class="cs-timeline__content">
        <h4>Status system definition</h4>
        <p>I designed the complete taxonomy of order statuses before touching any screen. Without that clear structure, the system has no criterion, only appearance.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-timeline__item">
      <div class="cs-timeline__marker"></div>
      <div class="cs-timeline__content">
        <h4>Prototyping, validation and handoff</h4>
        <p>Iterative prototypes of the search interface, publishing form and order detail. Validation with real buyers from public agencies and documentation for development.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="cs-section">
  <div class="cs-section__label">Design</div>
  <h2 class="cs-section__title">A status system before any screen</h2>
  <div class="cs-prose">
    <p>Each status has its own meaning, semantic color and set of allowed actions. They are readable for both the buyer and the supplier equally, they are not just labels, they are behavioral contracts.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ca-states">
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--orden">Purchase order issued</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">The buyer selected a quote and issued the order. The supplier must accept within the deadline. Highest urgency status for the supplier.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--recibiendo">Published, receiving quotes</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">The purchase is active. Any enabled supplier can quote within the published deadline. Main opportunity status.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--segundo">Second call</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">There were not enough quotes in the first call. The deadline is extended, a second opportunity for suppliers.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--desierta">Deserted</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">No valid quotes were received. Terminal status, the buyer must start a new process.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--cancelada">Cancelled</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">The agency cancelled the purchase before completing the process. Can occur at any active stage.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ca-state">
      <div class="ca-state__pill ca-pill--cerrada">Closed, awaiting results</div>
      <p class="ca-state__desc">The deadline ended. The buyer is evaluating proposals. Waiting status for the supplier.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-callout">
    <p><strong>Key design decision:</strong> statuses define what the user can do at each moment. The color, text and available actions change according to the status. The system has criterion, not just appearance.</p>
  </div>
</section>
